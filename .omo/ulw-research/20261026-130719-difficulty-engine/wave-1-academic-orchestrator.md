## Sources
1. Zohaib 2024 SLR "Dynamic difficulty adjustment approaches in video games"
   (Multimedia Tools & Applications) — DDA optimizes PX by auto-modifying
   game aspects. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11042-024-18768-x
2. "Rethinking DDA for video game design" (Entertainment Computing 2024) —
   flow state as goal of most DDA studies.
   https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1875952124000314
3. "Exploring DDA Methods" (Virtual Worlds 2024).
   https://doi.org/10.3390/virtualworlds3020012
4. "Measuring Control to Dynamically Induce Flow in Tetris" (IEEE TG 2022) —
   CBR approach: match difficulty evolution of past sessions that produced
   flow in similar-skill players; 40-participant experiment, measurable flow
   impact via validated questionnaires. https://doi.org/10.1109/tg.2022.3182901
5. "DDA for Maximized Engagement" (WWW 2017) — heuristic prediction +
   intervention paradigm. https://doi.org/10.1145/3041021.3054170
6. Pre-registered study (2022): difficulty-skill balance did NOT affect
   engagement/enjoyment with AI-controlled difficulty — counter-evidence.
   https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/6wh9m
7. Oxford Research Encyclopedia: difficulty is perceptual (skill + design +
   context); optimal difficulty moderate but evidence mixed, often simple
   negative difficulty->experience relationship.
8. PMC6820328 — optimal item calibration under 2PL IRT; naive sampling
   around design points is not optimal.
